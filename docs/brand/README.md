## Key facts at a glance (from the brochure)

- **Tagline:** Where the Himalaya Slows Time · A Boutique Himalayan Retreat
- **Name:** from *Arka*, the sun
- **Rooms:** 25 rooms across four blocks named after peaks: Nanda Kot, Nanda Devi, Trishul, Panchachuli
- **Room types:** Himalayan Panorama Room · Panoramic Balcony Room · Private Deck Residence · Arkaya Signature Suite (private deck, balcony, living space, dry kitchen)
- **Dining:** 180° Restobar, 65 seats, breakfast/lunch/dinner. Global comfort with regional inspiration. In-house bakery, Himalayan herb mocktails, children's menu.
- **Spaces:** Himadri Lounge (reading, library) · Summit Studio (gym) · Buransh Spa · Bugyal orchard garden · Shikhar Hall (meetings and events)
- **Decks:** Phool Dei (sunrise) · Monal · Oakwood (evening/sunset) · Brahma Kamal (contemplative)
- **Signature experiences:** sunrise Himalayan tea, orchard walks (apple, peach, plum, persimmon, walnut), sunset gatherings, oak and rhododendron forest trails, village and artisan visits
- **Nearby:** Mukteshwar Temple, Kainchi Dham, Jageshwar Dham, Bhimtal Lake
- **Pet friendly**
- **Sustainability:** solar, composting, water conservation. Water is purified and alkalized on site (Naula-inspired), and there are no plastic bottles ("Refill freely. Drink mindfully. Respect water.")
- **Address:** Dhanachuli Patti, Tehsil Dhari, Mukteshwar, Nainital – 263136
- **Travel:** Delhi 7–8 h · Kathgodam railway station 1.5–2 h · Pantnagar Airport 2.5 h
- **Contact:** +91 88698 17528 · fom@arkayamukteshwar.com · www.arkayamukteshwar.com

## Where the brief and the brochure differ

Go with the brochure unless the client confirms otherwise.

| Topic | Brand brief says | Brochure says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Closing line | "…a place to **align with the Himalaya**." | "…a place to **slow down, breathe deeply, and reconnect with the mountains**." |
| Restobar cuisine | "Global and Kumaoni flavours" | "Global comfort with regional inspiration" |
| Recreation | Archery, shooting, plus partner ziplining, sky cycling and waterfall swimming | Not mentioned. Confirm with the property before promoting. |
| Bonfires and stargazing | Listed as experiences | Not mentioned. Confirm before promoting. |
| Room count and types | Not given | 25 rooms, 4 types (see above) |
| Pet friendly, sustainability, Arka = sun | Not covered | Covered (see above) |
| Block name | "Nandakot" | "Nanda Kot" on the rooms page and the on-site sign, "Nandakot" on page 2. Use **Nanda Kot** to match the signage. |
