# Arkaya Mukteshwar — Brochure Text (official)

Full text of the 15-page Arkaya brochure, in page order. Page numbers and logo/flower artwork are left out. This is the official source for property facts.

## Page 1 (Cover)

**ARKAYA MUKTESHWAR**
Where the Himalaya Slows Time
A BOUTIQUE HIMALAYAN RETREAT

## Page 2

**ARKAYA MUKTESHWAR**

Arkaya Mukteshwar is a boutique Himalayan retreat where thoughtful luxury, nature, and mountain stillness come together to create a deeply restorative experience.

Perched among the quiet forests of Mukteshwar, the retreat unfolds gently with panoramic views, orchard gardens, and spaces designed for reflection and renewal.

Inspired by the great Himalayan peaks — Nanda Devi, Nandakot, Trishul, and Panchachuli — Arkaya invites guests to reconnect with nature and experience the quiet elegance of the mountains.

## Page 3

**The Arkaya Story**

Named after Arka — the sun, Arkaya reflects the natural rhythm of the day.

From the first golden glow of sunrise across distant peaks to the calm silence of twilight settling over the valleys, every moment follows the gentle cadence of the Himalaya.

Guided by sustainability and mountain wisdom, Arkaya embraces solar energy, mindful water practices, and architecture that lives lightly upon the land.

*Arkaya is not simply a place to stay. It is a place to slow down, breathe deeply, and reconnect with the mountains.*

## Page 4

**Mukteshwar**

Set in the serene Kumaon region of Uttarakhand, Mukteshwar is known for its pristine forests, spiritual heritage, and sweeping Himalayan panoramas.

From this peaceful hilltop, the majestic peaks of Nanda Devi, Panchachuli, and Trishul rise across the horizon.

The region offers a rare blend of nature, culture, and timeless mountain life, making it one of the most serene destinations in the Himalaya.

## Page 5

**The Arkaya Experience**

Arkaya has been envisioned as a layered Himalayan sanctuary where each space offers a distinct experience of the mountains.

Guests may wander through the Bugyal orchard garden, relax on scenic decks overlooking vast valleys, rejuvenate at Buransh Spa, or gather in the warm ambiance of Himadri Lounge.

(Four room and lounge photos, no other text.)

## Page 6

**Accommodation**

Arkaya offers 25 thoughtfully designed rooms across four residential blocks:
NANDA KOT · NANDA DEVI · TRISHUL · PANCHACHULI

Each residence frames expansive Himalayan views while offering quiet comfort and understated luxury.

**OUR ROOMS**

- **HIMALAYAN PANORAMA ROOM:** Spacious king rooms with sweeping Himalayan views.
- **PANORAMIC BALCONY ROOM:** Rooms with private balconies ideal for sunrise tea and sunset reflections.
- **PRIVATE DECK RESIDENCE:** Rooms featuring both balcony and private deck with uninterrupted valley vistas.
- **ARKAYA SIGNATURE SUITE:** Our most refined residence featuring a private deck, balcony, living space, and dry kitchen.

## Page 7

**180° RESTOBAR**

A panoramic dining experience overlooking Himalayan peaks and layered valleys.

The 65-seat restaurant offers a relaxed yet refined atmosphere for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Our culinary philosophy blends global comfort with regional inspiration.

**HIGHLIGHTS INCLUDE:**

- Fresh breads and desserts from the in-house bakery
- Himalayan herb mocktails
- A thoughtfully curated children's menu

Dining at Arkaya becomes an experience of flavor, view, and atmosphere.

## Page 8

**Himadri Lounge**

A welcoming space for reflection, reading, and quiet conversations.

Warm interiors, soft light, and expansive Himalayan views create an atmosphere where time slows naturally.

## Page 9

**Summit Studio**

A space to stay active, energised, and in rhythm with your routine.

Even while travelling, your wellness routine doesn't have to stop. The Fitness Centre at Arkaya Mukteshwar offers a thoughtfully equipped space for guests to maintain their fitness, from focused strength sessions to invigorating cardio workouts.

With modern equipment, a comfortable setting, and a calm atmosphere, it is designed to help you move, refresh, and feel your best throughout your stay.

Stay active. Feel refreshed. Keep your routine going.

**The Buransh Spa**

Named after the vibrant Himalayan Buransh flower, the spa offers therapies designed to restore balance and wellbeing.

Within this quiet sanctuary, the mountain calm becomes a personal journey of renewal.

## Page 10

**Scenic Deck Experiences**

Arkaya's open-air decks celebrate the changing moods of the Himalaya.

- **Phool Dei Deck:** A sunrise deck inspired by the spring festival of blossoms
- **Monal Deck:** Named after the Himalayan Monal, reflecting the vibrant beauty of the mountains.
- **Oakwood Deck:** An evening retreat framed by ancient oak trees and golden sunsets.
- **Brahma Kamal Deck:** A contemplative space inspired by the sacred Himalayan bloom.

## Page 11

**Signature Experiences**

Arkaya curates meaningful experiences that deepen the connection with the mountains.

- Sunrise Himalayan Tea on panoramic decks.
- Orchard walks through apple, peach, plum, persimmon, and walnut trees.
- Sunset gatherings overlooking vast valleys.
- Forest trails through oak and rhododendron landscapes.
- Village visits and local artisan encounters.

Each experience reflects the slow rhythm of mountain life.

## Page 12

**Local Experiences**

Mukteshwar offers immersive journeys into Kumaoni culture and nature.

- Village walks through traditional hamlets
- Visits to local artisan markets
- Nature trails and short treks
- Seasonal orchard harvest experiences

**Nearby Destinations**

Arkaya provides convenient access to several renowned destinations.

- **Mukteshwar Temple:** A historic Shiva temple with panoramic Himalayan views.
- **Kainchi Dham:** The revered ashram of Neem Karoli Baba.
- **Jageshwar Dham:** An ancient temple complex nestled within deodar forests.
- **Bhimtal Lake:** A scenic lake destination known for boating and mountain views.

## Page 13

Arkaya provides an inspiring setting for curated gatherings and retreats.

- **Corporate Offsites:** A refreshing environment where creativity and clarity flourish.
- **Wellness Retreats:** Yoga, meditation, and holistic programs in the tranquil Himalayan atmosphere.
- **Private Celebrations:** Birthdays, anniversaries, and intimate celebrations set against breathtaking mountain landscapes.
- **Shikhar Hall:** An elegant space for meetings, gatherings and intimate celebrations.

## Page 14

**Pet Friendly**

Arkaya warmly welcomes pets, ensuring every member of the family enjoys the mountain retreat experience.

**Sustainability & Water Story**

Arkaya follows a mindful approach to hospitality through:

- Solar energy systems
- Composting and waste reduction
- Responsible water conservation

Inspired by the traditional Himalayan Naula water systems, Arkaya purifies and alkalizes water on site.

Plastic bottles are intentionally avoided.

Refill freely. Drink mindfully.
Respect water.
